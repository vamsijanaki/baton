Fair use

For one person and their own accounts.

Baton organises work across accounts you already have. It doesn't change what any plan allows, and it isn't a way to get more usage.

Do

  • Use it with your own accounts, on your own Mac.
  • Use the right account for the right work: company code on work, side projects on personal.
  • Follow your employer's policy on which accounts and tools you may use.

Don't

  • Share an account with other people, or use someone else's.
  • Rotate several personal plans to get around usage limits.
  • Continue with another account after one is suspended or banned.
  • Resell access or run Baton as a service for others.
